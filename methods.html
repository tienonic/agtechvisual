Sources and methods

Home offers two public datasets. The agricultural technology collection contains 2,695 company records. The AI business map contains 5,715 source records plus 8 named relationship endpoints and 407 saved connections. Selecting both keeps each dataset's records and connections separate; it creates no cross-dataset relationships. AI import provenance records the exact imported files and hashes.

This catalog preserves the company records in the existing public agricultural technology reader. It is a research collection, not a complete registry. Categories and descriptions retain their saved values, including incomplete descriptions and possible classification errors.

  • Each cell represents one saved record. Categories and countries organize the map. No company relationships were supplied, so this dataset has no connection edges. City heights are flat when there are no connections.
  • Live, Dead and Not recorded describe the saved website observation. They do not describe whether a company operates today. Observation dates were absent from the public reader.
  • Funding is the sum of saved round amounts in USD, not valuation. Individual rounds and total-raised estimates can overlap. Missing positive amounts mean no amount was saved. Review the original rounds before making comparisons.
  • Every original record field is available in the lazy source evidence panel. Source labels are collector names; per-label source URLs and database IDs were not in the public reader.
  • Record IDs use the exact saved name and a duplicate ordinal. Names are not merged. A changed name creates a new ID; duplicate order follows the saved source order.

Original analytical reader retains Global, US and California filters, Classified, Funded and All filters, and Funding and Count sizing. Funding sizing follows that reader's logarithmic scaling; compare exact USD values in record details.
