Each cell is one record, and every record gets the same area. A larger block holds more records; size does not measure value. Blocks follow each dataset's groups and subgroups.

Lines are connections recorded in the source data. Solid lines are observed; dashed lines are inferred comparisons. Connections never join two datasets.

Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, and click a record to select it. Click a group title to zoom to it. In the city, drag to orbit and right-drag or Shift-drag to pan.

Keys: arrows move between records, Shift and an arrow pans or orbits, Enter opens the record in the library, Escape clears the selection, / searches, and 1, 2 and 3 switch views.
